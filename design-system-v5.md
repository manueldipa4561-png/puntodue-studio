# Dual Field v5

- Identity: two abstract forms, one system.
- Never animate or present normal-font `PD`, `P / D`, `P/D`, or `P↔D` as an identity motif.
- Typography: primarily grotesk/sans.
- Palette: cold ivory, obsidian, muted moss accent.
- Corners: sharp by default.
- Motion: motivated, reduced-motion aware.
- Interactive 3D: abstract sculptural forms only; no ordinary letter assembly.
